About

Nice to meet you. I'm a curious designer who believes everyone needs a little color in their life.

Three words I'd pick for myself: energetic, adaptive, and responsive. A background in graphic design and animation widens what I can put on the artboard and helps me sharpen the experience. Above all, I want people to walk away with a meaningful solution, and to actually enjoy solving their problem along the way.

I've been at IBM in Software under Data & AI for over five years now (wow!), working in enterprise design and new product development across Data Virtualization, Db2 Warehouse on Cloud, and watsonx.data. These days my focus is product-led growth and cross-product collaboration for platform experiences.

Outside of design, you'll find me dancing, dragging friends to new food spots, animating, and wholeheartedly selling my soul to YouTube. Ask me for a recommendation and I'll have a channel in any field, I promise. Otherwise, I'm probably running off to my next project.
